# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation free of harassment and welcoming regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Use welcoming language, respect different viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy. Harassment, insults, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
